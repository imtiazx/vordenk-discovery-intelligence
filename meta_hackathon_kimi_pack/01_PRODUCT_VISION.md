# Vordenk — Product Vision

## Vision

**Vordenk is an AI-native Enterprise Transformation Operating System that helps organisations move from messy business conversations to validated understanding, designed change, coordinated delivery, and measurable outcomes.**

The live consulting copilot is the first entry point, not the entire product.

## The problem from the founder's firsthand experience

As an AI consultant, the founder has experienced how difficult it is to listen carefully during complex client workshops while also understanding unfamiliar business processes, noticing dependencies, asking the right questions, and documenting requirements. Important details can be missed; evidence and decisions get scattered across meetings, documents, and stakeholders; and turning conversations into validated process maps and executable delivery plans requires substantial manual effort.

Typical meeting assistants focus on transcripts, summaries, and action items. Vordenk should help build a trustworthy model of the client's business and guide the next steps in transformation.

## Long-term lifecycle

1. **Engage:** understand the opportunity, objectives, stakeholders, scope, constraints, and success measures.
2. **Discover:** gather conversation and document evidence; understand current processes, systems, roles, rules, exceptions, and pain points.
3. **Diagnose:** identify root causes, bottlenecks, handoff failures, rework, risks, dependencies, and value leakage.
4. **Design:** create and compare TO-BE processes, solution alternatives, architecture options, data flows, and interactive prototypes.
5. **Mobilise:** convert approved understanding into requirements, prioritised backlogs, ownership, estimates, risks, plans, and acceptance criteria.
6. **Deliver:** maintain traceability through implementation, change impact, tests, UAT, issues, releases, and operational readiness.
7. **Realise value:** compare baselines with actual outcomes, adoption, cost, quality, time, and remaining gaps.

## Initial product wedge: Vordenk Discovery Intelligence

A consultant creates an engagement and runs multiple discovery sessions. Vordenk processes permitted live audio, recorded audio, or supplied transcripts and can reference authorised slides, whiteboard photos, process documents, or other artifacts. It extracts business knowledge into a persistent model, then periodically synthesises what is understood, what is uncertain, which statements conflict, which questions matter next, and what visual would help the group validate its understanding.

The consultant can present an editable process map or discovery canvas, gather client corrections, and resume later sessions without losing the validated context. The knowledge created here becomes the foundation for later diagnosis, solution design, requirements, delivery, and value tracking.

## Core principles

- **Evidence over assumptions:** material claims link to their original source, time, and session where feasible.
- **Persistent Transformation Memory:** facts, processes, decisions, questions, requirements, and risks persist across sessions and versions.
- **Status integrity:** keep Extracted, Inferred, Validated, and Decision distinct.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** people validate important information and approve consequential actions.
- **Consultant agency:** Vordenk suggests; the consultant decides what to ask, display, or share.
- **Explainable visuals:** diagrams are rendered from structured models and can be edited; they are not merely decorative AI images.
- **Multimodal context:** verbal conversation can be reconciled with approved visual/document evidence.
- **Privacy and trust:** informed consent, minimised collection, appropriate access, retention, and deletion.
- **Value throughout the lifecycle:** outputs should be reusable downstream and tied to measurable business outcomes.
- **Evaluation over hype:** test the accuracy and utility of claims, diagrams, questions, latency, reliability, and cost.

## Knowledge statuses

- **Extracted:** directly present in a source, not necessarily validated by a stakeholder.
- **Inferred:** proposed or deduced by the system; it must remain visibly uncertain.
- **Validated:** confirmed by an authorised person or an explicitly documented validation procedure.
- **Decision:** a human decision with owner/approver, date, scope, rationale, and supporting evidence where available.

A Decision is its own object, not simply a higher confidence score.

## What Vordenk is not

- Not just meeting notes or an auto-summary tool.
- Not a basic RAG chatbot.
- Not a generic collection of agents without a coherent shared business model.
- Not an autonomous consultant that decides for people.
- Not a full lifecycle suite in the first hackathon release.
- Not committed forever to any single model provider or agent framework.

## Long-term measures of value

Potential measures include reduced preparation time, improved coverage of critical process steps and exceptions, percentage of material claims with valid evidence, useful question rate, contradiction detection, time to produce validated process maps and requirements, reduction in rework, improved traceability, and later, realised business outcomes. These are hypotheses until measured with representative users and data.
